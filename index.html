Questions, answered.

What is LicoUp?

An open-source, local-first Human-Agent Collaboration Client. It starts with conversations with the AI agents on your Mac and is growing into a messenger for people and agents.

About the product
Can I message other people today?

Not yet. Today's release focuses on local agents. Messaging with people, shared results and multi-device history are on the roadmap.

Current progress
Which agents does it work with?

13 built-in adapters, including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and OpenCode. Install and set up each agent first; its version and interface decide what works.

Check compatibility
Does local-first mean offline?

Not quite. Your conversations stay on your device, but an agent you pick may use an online model, which sees what you send it.

Privacy boundaries
Human-agent or multi-agent collaboration?

Both fit. Human-agent collaboration keeps you in charge of goals, context and approvals; multi-agent collaboration means several agents share the work. One conversation can involve both.

The collaboration model
Can I download it today?

Yes. v0.1.2 is available for Macs with Apple silicon. The release page has the DMG and verification files.
