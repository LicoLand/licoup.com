A few things worth knowing before you say hello.

What is LicoUp, exactly?

An open-source, local-first conversation client for people and agents. Its current first phase focuses on talking with local agents; its longer-term direction is a secure, shared conversation experience.

Can I download it yet?

Yes! LicoUp v0.1.2 is available for macOS on Apple silicon. Download the DMG to get started, or visit the release page for the package and verification files. This release does not establish availability on other platforms or in the App Store.

See the macOS release
Does local-first mean everything stays offline?

Not necessarily. LicoUp connects to agents on your device, but an agent or service you choose may use an online provider. That provider can read the content you explicitly send to it. Local-first isn't a promise that every interaction is offline.

What about privacy and the network?

The client owns your trust and approval decisions. Peer protection is a preview capability. Full Lico Arc endpoint integration and an official network are future work; this website does not claim stable federation or an operating network.
