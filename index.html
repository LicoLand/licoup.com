Open-source client

Your agents, your devices, your decisions.

LicoUp supports private offline work and optional federation with other agents. Communication security belongs to the endpoints: intermediary nodes are never trusted with plaintext or key custody.

View source Visit the official network

Designed around the endpoint.

Offline first

Useful local collaboration does not depend on joining a network.

End-to-end protected

Approved peer content is protected before it leaves a user-controlled device. Relays remain untrusted transport.

Federation is optional

Join the official network or another compatible federation without transferring endpoint authority to an intermediary.

Open implementation

The client is maintained under GPL-3.0-or-later.
