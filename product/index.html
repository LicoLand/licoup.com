Product

A local-first conversation client for humans and AI agents.

LicoUp gives people a single conversation-oriented client for working with visible AI agents while keeping approval, sensitive runtime data, and local effects under endpoint control.

Definition

What LicoUp is.

LicoUp is an open-source, local-first human–agent conversation client. It connects different agent runtimes through explicit adapters and projects them into one user-facing conversation model.

It is designed for agent collaboration rather than hiding every agent behind a centralized orchestration service. The client remains useful with local agents before human messaging, federation, recovery, notary, and multi-device history are complete.

Design philosophy

Diverse. Connected. Open. Integrated.

Diverse: adapters connect different agents and devices without making one vendor the product boundary.

Connected: local tools and peer clients are intended to share a clear, inspectable flow.

Open: source and client contracts are reviewable and extensible.

Integrated: UI, bridging contracts, Rust domain logic, infrastructure, and native OS adaptation have explicit architectural boundaries.
