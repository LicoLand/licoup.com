# LicoUp Website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose and Audience

This static website introduces LicoUp to individuals who want a more approachable
way to converse with their local agents. Visitors should understand the product,
explore an explicitly illustrative conversation, download the macOS release,
and find the public source.

## Authority and Constraints

This is a presentation brief, not a product specification. Product intent belongs
to [LicoUp PRODUCT.md](https://github.com/LicoLand/LicoUp/blob/nightly/PRODUCT.md).
Implementation and release facts belong to
[LicoUp STATUS.md](https://github.com/LicoLand/LicoUp/blob/nightly/docs/STATUS.md).
[GitHub Release v0.1.2](https://github.com/LicoLand/LicoUp/releases/tag/v0.1.2)
provides the released macOS Apple silicon DMG. That exact public release is the
authority for the download entry. The site must distinguish current local-agent
work from future federation and
network plans. Examples are synthetic. No runtime data is included.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing LicoUp logo. The authorized redesign uses a lively,
approachable consumer-product voice, expressive typography and rich, coherent
motion, informed by Awwwards award-winning sites. The remaining visual direction
is delegated to the designer. Chinese and English share one experience.

## Accessibility

Keep navigation and examples usable by keyboard and touch. Respect reduced
motion and preserve readable content when scripts or motion are unavailable.
